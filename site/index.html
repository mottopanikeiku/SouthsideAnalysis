    What the scores mean

    Louvain uses weighted edges; Leiden uses unweighted edges.

    Their modularity scores measure different objectives and should not be ranked against each other. Graph edges connect similar demographics, not necessarily adjacent places.

    I treated negative ACS estimate codes as missing before calculating rates, then median-imputed missing features. The map shows those corrected runs.

    No map tiles or external libraries. Geometry and labels load from local data.json. Census data describe groups of people, not individual records.
